Common Questions

Plans, safety & how it works

What is the difference between the Yearly and Monthly plans?

Both plans let you clean up to 5 devices. The Yearly plan is £49.99/year (saving 58%, or £4.16/month), while the Monthly plan is £9.99/month with no long-term commitment. You can cancel anytime in one click.

Can I get a file back if I clean it by mistake?

Yes! On Windows, Mac, and Linux, cleaned files are kept safely in SWEEPR's 7-Day Undo folder so you can put any file right back where it was in one click. On iPhone and Android, deleted photos stay in your phone's Recently Deleted album for 30 days.

How do the Battery Health Saver and Cloud Protection add-ons work?

Each optional add-on is £9.99/year. Battery Health Saver alerts you at 80% charge, pauses idle browser tabs, and cools down hot laptops. Cloud Protection gives you a password-locked folder for private files and automatically backs up important folders to OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox, or iCloud.
