SWEEPR Cleaner vs Wise Disk Cleaner

Wise Disk Cleaner is genuinely lightweight (~8.5 MB) and avoids registry bloat—making it one of the better legacy freeware cleaners. Here is where SWEEPR Cleaner goes further on rollback safety, modern app caches, and 5-device support.

QUICK VERDICT

Wise Disk Cleaner (by WiseCleaner / Lespeed Technology, independent of Gen Digital and Avast) earns credit for staying genuinely tiny (~8.5–8.6 MB installer), running on-demand without a heavy resident daemon, and—unlike many rivals—excluding risky registry cleaning. Its trade-offs are narrow scope and one-way deletion: there is no 7-day Undo Quarantine, no active process-lock detection, no duplicate finder, no app uninstaller, no file shredder, and Windows-only coverage with sibling prompts for Wise Care 365. SWEEPR Cleaner (6.52 MB native Rust) matches Wise’s lightweight footprint while adding 7-day rollback safety, audited rules for GPU/gaming/AI/24H2 caches, SHA-256 duplicates, and 5 cross-platform devices.

When to choose SWEEPR Cleaner

  • You want a 7-day Undo Quarantine and process-lock checks so deleting an active browser, game, or editor cache never breaks your session.
  • You need deep rules for multi-GB modern paths like NVIDIA DXCache, LiveKernelReports crash dumps, Logitech G HUB depots, and ComfyUI.
  • You want built-in SHA-256 Duplicate Finder, App Uninstaller & Updater, File Shredder, and 5-device coverage across Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, and Android.

When to keep or pick Wise Disk Cleaner

  • You want a 100% free personal Windows utility strictly for quick temp/browser junk sweeps, System Slimmer (trimming legacy Windows wallpapers/sample files), or Disk Defrag / SSD Trim.
  • You prefer portable ZIP execution or scheduled basic Windows cleaning, do not need Mac/mobile coverage, and accept permanent deletion with no rollback vault.

Both Are Under 9 MB—So What Is Actually Different?

Unlike bloated 90+ MB tune-up suites, both SWEEPR (6.52 MB) and Wise Disk Cleaner (~8.5–8.6 MB) respect disk space and leave risky registry cleaning out of disk cleanup. The real difference is engineering depth and reversibility: Wise relies on broad file-extension sweeps and Windows System Slimmer deletions with zero undo vault, whereas SWEEPR uses 380 audited path rules with active process-lock checks and a 7-day Undo Quarantine so recoverable caches can be restored in one click.

Modern GPU, Gaming & Multi-Device Cleanup Beyond Windows Temp

Wise Disk Cleaner stops at Windows temp, browser caches, System Slimmer, and drive defrag/trim. On modern PCs, however, 10–65 GB of real reclaimable bloat hides inside NVIDIA DXCache (2–14 GB), Windows LiveKernelReports crash dumps (1.5–10 GB), Logitech G HUB update depots, and Adobe Premiere Media Cache—while SWEEPR also covers your Mac, iPhone, Android, and Linux machines under one 5-device account.
