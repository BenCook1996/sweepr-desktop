1. No Bloatware, No Scareware, No Popups

The biggest complaint from PC enthusiasts regarding CCleaner is its persistent background agent and pop-up notifications urging users to upgrade or install partner software.

Sweepr operates on an Anti-Scareware philosophy. It runs when you open it, cleans what you authorize, and exits completely. It does not run background processes that consume CPU cycles, nor does it invent fake "Critical System Errors" to frighten users into upgrading.

2. Native Rust Performance vs. Legacy Bloat

Sweepr was built using Rust and Tauri, meaning it compiles to lightweight, memory-safe native binaries. The entire installer is ~12 MB, compared to CCleaner’s 85MB+ package.

Sweepr scans your disks using multi-threaded asynchronous I/O, cataloging browser caches (Chrome, Edge, Brave), app storage (Discord, Spotify, Steam), and hidden Windows temporary directories in under 60 seconds.

CCleaner charges extra for multi-device tiers. With Sweepr, one Pro license unlocks protection for your main desktop PC, your laptop, and your partner's or work computer (up to 3 PCs total), plus 2 smartphones (Android or iPhone).
