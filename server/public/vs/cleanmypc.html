What Happened to CleanMyPC After April 2025

Unlike CleanMyMac—which MacPaw continues to develop actively for macOS—CleanMyPC paused active feature work in late 2021 and was officially sunsetted on April 22, 2025. Existing subscribers received legacy lifetime builds, while new sales ended. That leaves older CleanMyPC rule sets blind to where Windows 11 24H2+ actually accumulates 10–50 GB of clutter: 24H2 checkpoint update staging, Edge WebView2 runtimes, DirectX shader caches, and local AI/game depots.

SWEEPR Cleaner keeps CleanMyPC’s clean editorial clarity while adding actively audited paths—including safe guidance for large OS files like hiberfil.sys so you reclaim disk space without breaking Fast Startup unexpectedly—plus a 7-day Undo Quarantine for eligible cache deletions.

Migrating From CleanMyPC Without Losing Mac or Mobile Coverage

Many CleanMyPC owners originally picked MacPaw because they ran a Windows desktop alongside a MacBook or iPhone. SWEEPR’s unified 5-device Pro license bridges that exact mixed household: one account activates Windows 10/11, macOS 11+ (.dmg), iOS, Android, and Linux (.AppImage) from a lightweight native codebase with zero background telemetry.
