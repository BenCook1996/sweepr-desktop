SWEEPR vs Pearcleaner

Pearcleaner is a fast, open-source Swift app uninstaller for Mac. When you need to clean 20–60 GB of caches inside apps you actually keep installed—or cover your Windows PC too—here is how the two compare.

QUICK VERDICT

Pearcleaner is a great free macOS utility (~6 MB) for cleanly deleting an .app bundle along with orphaned .plist preferences. SWEEPR Cleaner (also 6.52 MB native) solves the other 85% of disk bloat: cleaning gigabytes of caches inside apps you keep installed, finding byte-identical SHA-256 duplicates, protecting cleans inside a 7-day Undo Quarantine, and supporting 5 devices across macOS, Windows 11/10, iOS, and Android.

  • Your SSD is full because of active apps you use daily (Discord, Chrome, ComfyUI, Docker, Spotify, macOS System Data)—not apps you want to uninstall.

Why Uninstalling Apps Only Solves Part of SSD Bloat

Developer toolchains (ComfyUI temp outputs, Xcode DerivedData, Docker images) and everyday apps (Discord, Spotify) silently accumulate 30–80 GB while remaining installed. SWEEPR targets those safe caches without requiring you to uninstall anything.
