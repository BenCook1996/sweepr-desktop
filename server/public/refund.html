Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Our Promise: You are eligible for a full 100% refund within 14 days of any charge if you are not completely satisfied with SWEEPR Cleaner Pro—no hassle, no complicated forms.

1. 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

Every SWEEPR Cleaner subscription and introductory activation is backed by a full 14-day money-back guarantee. If SWEEPR Cleaner does not meet your expectations for any reason, simply request a refund within 14 calendar days of your initial purchase or renewal charge, and we will return 100% of the payment to your original payment method.

2. How to Cancel Your Subscription Anytime (1-Click)

You are in full control of your subscription at all times. To cancel automatic renewal before your next billing date:

  1. Visit the SWEEPR Cleaner Customer Portal and sign in with your email address (we will send you an instant 6-digit login code).
  2. Click Manage Billing & Payments or Cancel Subscription on your dashboard.
  3. Your cancellation takes effect immediately, and you will never be billed again.

3. How to Request a Full Refund

To request a refund within 14 days of a charge, visit our Support Page or submit a ticket with the email address used at checkout. Our customer support team handles refund requests around the clock.

4. Processing Timelines & Payment Methods

Once approved, refunds are submitted immediately via our payment processor, Stripe. Depending on your financial institution or card issuer, funds will typically appear back on your account within:

  • Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex): 3–5 business days.
  • Apple Pay & Google Pay: 1–3 business days depending on linked bank.
  • International Transactions: Up to 7 business days depending on local currency conversion.

5. Device Transfers & Multi-PC Usage

Before canceling, remember that every SWEEPR Cleaner Pro license covers up to three (3) active Desktop PCs. If you are replacing a computer or reinstalling Windows, you do not need to purchase a new license—you can easily transfer your active key through the Customer Portal at any time.

6. Contacting Billing Support

If you have any questions regarding your invoice, charges, or need assistance modifying your plan, reach out directly via our 24/7 Support Desk.
