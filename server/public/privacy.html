Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Effective Date: October 2026 | Version 2.4

1. Local-First Processing Architecture

SWEEPR Cleaner is engineered with an uncompromising local-first privacy architecture. All scanning, junk file analysis, duplicate file comparison, and permanent shredding occur 100% locally on your device via our native Rust engine. We never read, upload, inspect, or transmit your personal documents, photos, browsing history, or sensitive application state to remote servers.

2. Information Collected on Our Website

When you interact with our website (sweeprcleaner.com) or subscribe to SWEEPR Cleaner Pro, we collect the minimal information necessary to deliver and maintain your license:

  • Account & Licensing Information: Your registered email address, subscription plan tier, and an anonymous cryptographic device identifier to enforce your 3-PC license limit.
  • Billing Information: Payment processing is handled exclusively by Stripe (PCI-DSS Level 1 certified). We never store, process, or have access to full credit card numbers or banking passwords.
  • Support Communications: Name, email address, and message contents submitted when you contact technical support or request license assistance.

3. Zero Third-Party Advertising & Tracking

SWEEPR Cleaner does not include third-party advertising networks, affiliate tracking SDKs, or behavioral data brokers. Unlike legacy cleaners, we do not monetize user data or inject promotional banners into your desktop operating system.

4. Data Retention & Security Standards

We implement industry-standard security measures including Ed25519 asymmetric signature verification, TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, and AES-256 encryption at rest. Customer licensing records are retained only for the active lifecycle of your account plus standard financial audit compliance periods.

5. Your GDPR, UK DPA & CCPA Rights

Under applicable data protection laws (including the EU GDPR, UK Data Protection Act 2018, and California Consumer Privacy Act), you retain the right to access, rectify, export, or permanently delete your account data. You can exercise self-service account deletion at any time via the Customer Portal (/login) or by contacting our Data Protection Officer.

6. Contact & Inquiries

For any privacy questions or data subject access requests, contact our privacy engineering team through our Support Center.
