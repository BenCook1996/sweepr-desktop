Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Effective Date: November 2026 Version 2.5

1. Local-First Processing Architecture (What We Never Collect)

SWEEPR Cleaner is engineered with an uncompromising local-first privacy architecture. All system scanning, junk file analysis, duplicate file comparison, registry/startup inspection, and permanent file shredding occur 100% locally on your device via our native Rust engine (you can also test paths in your browser with our Safe-to-Delete Path Checker). Desktop Zero-Telemetry / Offline Mode (telemetry_enabled = false) is enabled by default, so optional anonymized desktop app/crash diagnostics (disclosed in Section 2) are sent only if you explicitly opt in under Settings. Even when opted in or using account/web features, the desktop application never reads, uploads, inspects, or transmits your personal file paths, file contents, documents, photos, browsing history, or sensitive application files to remote servers.

2. User Data We Collect

Yes, we collect limited user data on our website (sweeprcleaner.com), Customer Portal, licensing servers, and optional opt-in anonymized desktop application diagnostics. We practice strict data minimization and collect only the data required to deliver and maintain your software license, process payments, diagnose crashes when opted in, provide support, and measure aggregate website/app performance:

  • Account, Licensing & Authentication Data: When you purchase a subscription, activate a trial, or sign in to the Customer Portal (/login), we collect your registered email address, subscription plan tier, one-time passcode (OTP) login verification records, and a one-way cryptographic hardware/device identifier hash used solely to enforce your license seat limit of up to 5 devices across Windows, macOS, Android, and iOS (Safari web companion / TestFlight beta).
  • Billing, Currency & Payment Data: When you check out, we collect your billing country, currency preference, and transaction status. Web payment processing is handled directly by Stripe, Inc. or PayPal under PCI-DSS Level 1 certification, and Android in-app subscriptions (com.sweepr.app) are processed by Google Play and synced via RevenueCat. SWEEPR never stores, processes, or has access to your raw credit card numbers, CVV, or banking passwords.
  • Desktop App Telemetry & Sanitized Crash Reports (POST /v1/telemetry/event & POST /v1/telemetry/crash): Zero-Telemetry / Offline Mode is enabled by default (telemetry_enabled = false), meaning the desktop application makes zero background telemetry or crash uploads unless you explicitly turn telemetry on in Settings. When opted in, the desktop application sends anonymized lifecycle/funnel events (POST /v1/telemetry/event) containing event name, OS platform, app version, random/cryptographic device_id, attribution ID, and aggregate byte counts or per-rule aggregate cache sizes (used for k-anonymized storage benchmarks)—never personal file paths or file contents. If an unexpected panic occurs while opted in, POST /v1/telemetry/crash sends the OS, app version, relative source location, and sanitized panic text with personal filesystem paths (<redacted_path>) and email addresses (<redacted_email>) stripped locally before transmission.
  • Website Analytics, Cookies & Attribution Data: When you visit sweeprcleaner.com, we collect aggregate website telemetry (pages visited, referral URLs, browser/OS type, approximate country derived from IP address, and marketing conversion events) via first-party session cookies and the third-party analytics/attribution providers listed in Section 4.
  • Support Communications: When you contact technical support or request license assistance via our Support Center or email, we collect your name, email address, and message contents.

3. How We Use Artificial Intelligence (AI) & Smart Optimization Heuristics

Yes, we use Artificial Intelligence (AI) and machine-assisted heuristic classification systems to power smart optimization features and customer support workflows:

  • AI-Assisted Junk, Cache & Bloatware Classification: SWEEPR Cleaner uses AI-trained heuristic rules and signature databases that execute 100% locally on your computer to distinguish safe-to-remove application caches, orphaned installer residuals, and startup bloatware from essential operating system and user files.
  • Smart Duplicate & Clutter Scoring: Local heuristic algorithms evaluate file hashes, timestamps, and directory structures on your device to recommend safe duplicate cleanup without uploading your files to the cloud.
  • AI-Assisted Customer Support & Diagnostics: We may use AI-assisted tools internally to categorize support tickets, draft troubleshooting guidance, and maintain signature definitions for new Windows/macOS application caches.
  • Strict AI Privacy Guarantee (Zero AI Training on User Files): Your personal files, documents, photos, and local directory trees never leave your device, are never transmitted to external AI providers, and are never used to train or fine-tune external AI or large language models (LLMs).

4. Third-Party Data Collectors, Analytics & Named Sub-Processors

Yes, we use third-party data collectors and infrastructure sub-processors on our website (sweeprcleaner.com) and billing backend. The SWEEPR Cleaner desktop application itself contains zero third-party ad networks or behavioral data brokers and communicates only with first-party sweeprcleaner.com endpoints for licensing, anonymized app diagnostics, and sanitized crash reports. On our website, checkout, and licensing infrastructure, we share or process limited data only with the following named third parties:

  • Google LLC (Google Analytics 4): Third-party website analytics collector used on sweeprcleaner.com to measure aggregate visitor traffic, page views, and checkout conversion performance.
  • Stripe, Inc., PayPal (Europe) S.à r.l. et Cie, S.C.A. & Google Play / RevenueCat: PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processors that collect and process your payment card details, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link, PayPal transactions, Google Play in-app subscriptions (com.sweepr.app), billing country, and anti-fraud signals during checkout.
  • Advertising & Conversion Attribution Providers (Website Only): On our public marketing and checkout pages, we may load conversion attribution tags operated by Microsoft Corporation (Microsoft Bing Ads — bat.bing.com), Reddit, Inc., Meta Platforms, Inc., and OpenAI, L.L.C. solely to measure the effectiveness of our paid marketing campaigns.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. & Railway Corp.: Cloud infrastructure, Content Delivery Network (CDN), DDoS protection, TLS 1.3 encryption, and backend hosting providers that process incoming HTTP requests and IP addresses to serve our website and licensing API securely.
  • Country.is (api.country.is) & GeoJS (get.geojs.io): Lightweight IP-to-country lookup services used on the website pricing page solely to display regional currency across 30 supported regional currencies (including GBP, USD, EUR, CAD, AUD, JPY, and more).
  • Have I Been Pwned (api.pwnedpasswords.com k-Anonymity API): When using the optional Breach & Password Hygiene check, only a truncated 5-character SHA-1 hash prefix is queried via k-anonymity so your full password or credential never leaves your device.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to data brokers or third-party marketers.

5. Data Retention & Security Standards

We implement industry-standard security measures including Ed25519 asymmetric signature verification, TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, and AES-256 encryption at rest. Customer licensing records are retained only for the active lifecycle of your account plus standard UK/EU financial audit compliance periods.

6. Your GDPR, UK DPA & CCPA Rights

Under applicable data protection laws (including the EU GDPR, UK Data Protection Act 2018, and California Consumer Privacy Act), you retain the right to access, rectify, export, opt out of website analytics cookies via Cookie Preferences, or permanently delete your account data. You can exercise self-service account deletion at any time on our /delete-account page, via the Customer Portal (/login), or by contacting developer Benjamin Cook directly.

7. Contact & Inquiries

For any privacy questions or data subject access requests, contact Benjamin Cook (sole trader, United Kingdom, trading as SWEEPR Cleaner; correspondence address: 87 Upper Brighton Road, Worthing, West Sussex; see About) through our Support Center or at support@sweeprcleaner.com (typical response within 1 business day (UK time)).
