Frequently Answered Questions

How do I activate my Pro license after purchase?

Immediately following checkout, we generate a unique 6-digit Subscription Key sent to your email. Launch SWEEPR Cleaner on your PC, click the 'Account' icon in the left navigation, enter your email and 6-digit code, and click 'Activate Pro'. All Pro features unlock instantly on up to 3 PCs.

How does the 7-day safety undo feature work?

Before deleting temporary cache or uninstalled application files, SWEEPR Cleaner creates an encrypted, compressed recovery manifest. If you ever need to restore cleared files within 7 days, open the app, navigate to 'History & Rollback', and click 'Undo Last Clean'.

Can I transfer my license to a new or formatted computer?

Yes. Every Pro license covers up to 3 active Desktop PCs. If you upgrade your PC or reinstall Windows, simply log into the Customer Portal at /login to view your active devices or enter your key on the new machine.

How do I request a refund under the 14-day guarantee?

We provide a 100% no-quibble refund within 14 calendar days of your purchase. Simply fill out the Contact Support form above with your purchase email address, and our team will issue the refund through Stripe within 24 hours.
