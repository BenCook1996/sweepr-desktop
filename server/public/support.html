Frequently Answered Questions

How do I activate my Pro license after purchase?

Immediately following checkout, we generate a unique SWEEPR-PRO-... Subscription Key sent to your email. Launch SWEEPR Cleaner on your PC, click the ‘Account’ icon in the left navigation, enter your email and full SWEEPR-PRO-... Subscription Key, and click ‘Activate Pro’. All Pro features unlock instantly so you can clean up to 5 devices.

How does the 7-day safety undo feature work?

Before deleting temporary cache or uninstalled application files, SWEEPR Cleaner creates an encrypted, compressed recovery manifest. If you ever need to restore cleared files within 7 days, open the app, navigate to ‘History & Rollback’, and click ‘Undo Last Clean’.

Can I transfer my license to a new or formatted computer?

Yes. Every Pro license covers up to 5 devices across Windows, macOS, iOS, and Android. If you upgrade your PC or reinstall Windows, simply log into the Customer Portal at /login to view your active devices or enter your key on the new machine.

How do I request a refund under the 14-day guarantee?

We provide a 100% no-quibble refund within 14 calendar days of your purchase. Simply fill out the Contact Support form above with your purchase email address, and our team will issue the refund through Stripe within 24 hours.

Which Windows versions and architectures are supported?

SWEEPR Cleaner is engineered specifically for Windows 11 (including versions 24H2, 23H2, and earlier) as well as Windows 10 (both 64-bit and 32-bit). It is compiled as a standalone, ultra-lean native binary (6.52 MB) with zero runtime dependencies like .NET or Java.

Do I need administrator permissions to run SWEEPR Cleaner?

Everyday cleaning for user-level temporary files, application caches, browser cookies, and duplicate files requires no administrative elevation. For system-level operations such as purging Windows Update staging files or old error dumps, Windows will display a standard UAC prompt so you retain complete control.
