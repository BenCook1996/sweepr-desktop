## Part 1: How Google Search Ads Work (In Plain English)

Before clicking anything in Google Ads, here is the simple hierarchy of how a campaign works:

1. **Campaign (The Rules & Budget):** Where you set your daily budget (£10–£20/day), which countries see your ads (e.g., UK), and which devices see your ads (Desktop Windows/Mac computers only).
2. **Ad Group (The Topic Folder):** Inside your campaign, you create an Ad Group for a specific group of searchers—in our case, people searching for **CCleaner Alternatives**.
3. **Keywords (The Triggers):** The exact phrases people type into Google (like `ccleaner alternative`) that trigger your ad to appear.
   - **`[exact match]` (Square Brackets):** Shows your ad *only* when someone searches that exact meaning (e.g., `[ccleaner alternative]`). Highest profit, zero wasted money.
   - **`"phrase match"` (Quotation Marks):** Shows your ad when someone's search *includes* your phrase (e.g., `"replace ccleaner"` matches *"best way to replace ccleaner on windows 11"*).
   - **Never use Broad Match (no brackets or quotes):** If you type `ccleaner alternative` with no brackets or quotes, Google will waste your budget showing ads on unrelated searches like *"how to clean a computer screen"*.
4. **Negative Keywords (The Shield):** Words you tell Google to **block** (like `free`, `crack`, `torrent`, `vacuum`, `carpet`) so you never pay for clicks from pirates or people looking for household cleaners.
5. **Responsive Search Ad (The Ad Copy):** You give Google up to **15 short headlines** and **4 descriptions**, and Google automatically mixes and matches the best combination for each searcher and sends them to `https://sweeprcleaner.com/vs/ccleaner`.

## Part 8: Your Weekly 5-Minute Checklist (How to Manage It)

You only need to check Google Ads twice a week for 5 minutes:
1. **Check the "Search Terms" Report:** Go to **Campaigns -> Insights and reports -> Search terms**. This shows the *actual* sentences people typed before clicking your ad. If you see anything irrelevant, check the box next to it and click **"Add as negative keyword"**.
2. **Watch Your 3 Key Numbers:**
   - **CTR (Click-Through Rate):** Aim for **> 5%** (shows your ad copy is compelling).
   - **Avg. CPC (Cost Per Click):** Should be around **£0.55 – £1.10**.
   - **Cost / Conv. (Cost Per Purchase or Trial Start):** Your break-even Day-7 target is **£7.00**, and your profitable 30-day scaling target is **£10.00 – £12.00** per £1.99 starter (or **£18 – £22** per retained £9.99/mo subscriber).
